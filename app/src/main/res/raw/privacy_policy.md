# Desk Days privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Desk Days is an office-attendance tracker. It has no servers and no accounts, and the developer never receives your data.

## Location, including in the background

If you turn on automatic tracking, Desk Days uses your device's location, including when the app is closed or not in use (background location), to detect when you arrive at and leave the office area you set. It does this with Android's geofencing service.

Desk Days saves only:
- the location and radius of your office, which you choose, and
- the times you arrived at and left that area.

It does not record or keep where else you go.

You can turn automatic tracking off at any time and log days manually instead.

## Wi-Fi

If you turn on "Confirm with office Wi-Fi", Desk Days saves the name (SSID) and hardware address (BSSID) of your office network. While you're at the office, it checks about every 15 minutes whether your phone is connected to that network, and saves only the result (on or off the office network) with the time. It doesn't record other networks. Android only lets apps read Wi-Fi names when they have location permission.

## Where your data is stored

Everything is stored on your device. Android's own backup may include it in your device backup, under your Google account and Google's terms.

If you turn on Google Drive backup, Desk Days saves a copy of its data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Desk Days can read that folder; the developer has no access to it. You can delete the backup from within the app at any time.

## Address lookup and maps

When you set up your office, the address you type, or the point you choose on the map, may be sent to Google's geocoding and map services to show the map and convert between addresses and coordinates. This happens only during office setup.

## Tips

Optional tips are processed by Google Play. Desk Days receives only a confirmation that a purchase happened; it never sees your payment details.

## Sharing

Desk Days does not sell or share your data, and it contains no ads or analytics.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Desk Days deletes all data on the device. Delete a Drive backup from the app's Backup screen, or from your Google Drive settings under "Manage apps".
