Let Desk Days know when you're at the office

Desk Days uses your location in the background, even when the app is closed, to log when you arrive at and leave your office. That's how it counts your office days.

Only arrival and departure times at your office are saved, not where else you go
Stays on this phone, unless you turn on backup to your own Google Drive
Turn it off anytime in Settings and log days manually instead

On the next screen, choose Allow all the time so tracking works when the app is closed.
